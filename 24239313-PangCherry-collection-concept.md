Electricity is the topic I have selected. I consider electricity to be a very magical phenomenon that may be perceived in a variety of ways. It could be a tangible thing or energy that is detectable yet unseen to the naked sight. It can also be considered as an acquired "life," however this "life" is drastically different from our ordinary understanding of existence. Electricity is the basic lifeblood of innumerable electronic gadgets and a prerequisite for the maintenance of all electronic equipment. But this electronic life has the "special effect" of "resurrection after death." An electronic device will be in a state of "death" when its battery runs out completely and it is unable to react to the outside environment. However, its demise is just momentary; upon recharging, it will be given "new life." All of the previous "life" has been depleted and cannot be replenished in any manner. When we utilize electronic devices, that power will be lost. Our usage of it is causing it to vanish. Twenty records will be used by my archive to record the amount of electricity used, how people replace it, and what happens in the absence of electricity.

These 20 records have one thing in common: human intervention was always involved in the use and recharging of power, whether it was used or replenished. Human activity has always been the driving force behind the use of electricity; since the Second Industrial Revolution, often known as the Electrical Age, people have been in charge of how and where energy is used. As a result, my 20 albums are all about how people control and affect how electricity is used in various items and technologies, as well as how the electrical energy that the human body possesses affects both the human body and objects.

Unfortunately, due to power consumption or recharging, nearly all records are unable to capture the heat produced by electrical devices. Even with visuals, music, and text, it is challenging to capture the heat produced by electronic devices precisely. The global flow of electricity is another area that cannot be documented. Even though we know that electricity is charging my phone or computer, I am unable to see the flow and input of electrons toward the computer while charging since electricity is invisible to the unaided eye. I have not included this in any of my records.
